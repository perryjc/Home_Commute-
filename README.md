# Commute Home

## Game vibe:
> Our game will be a top down, retro, racing game. 
> Their will be infinite (?) side scrolling.
> The player will control a car.

## Music:
> Retro, 8-bit, Arcade, High Energy 

## Objectives:
> The player will drive increasingly faster while doging cars going at diffrent speeds.
> The score will increase as time goes on (like chrome dino game). We may add a feature
> Where a cut scene will play at a certain score, but most likely just infinite.
> There will also be a high score system. 

## Control:
> The player will control the car useing arrow keys. left and right for x-axis and
> up and down for y-axis.
